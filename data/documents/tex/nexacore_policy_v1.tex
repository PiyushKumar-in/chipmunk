\begin{abstract}
This policy defines employee transport eligibility, women's night-shift safety, shuttle and cab cut-offs, ad-hoc reimbursement limits, weekend lab-shift transport, visitor commute rules, and no-show penalties for NexaCore BioLabs India Pvt. Ltd. (Hyderabad Genome Valley). For scenarios not covered in this document, contact the NexaCore Transport Desk at \texttt{transport@nexacorebio.example.com}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Eligibility Criteria}
\subsection{Employee Tier Eligibility}
All permanent full-time employees across \textbf{Tiers A through D} as well as paid research \textbf{interns} are eligible for company shuttles and shared cabs. Third-party agency contractors and temporary lab technicians are excluded from company transport and must use their agency's commute arrangements.

\subsection{Distance and Geographic Radius}
Home-to-campus shared cabs serve residences located between \textbf{4 km and 30 km} from the Genome Valley campus. Point-to-point trunk shuttles operate from designated Hyderabad metro hubs (Miyapur, Ameerpet, and Secunderabad) regardless of home distance.

\subsection{Shift Eligibility}
Transport services align with NexaCore's three wet-lab and R\&D shifts:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Lab Shift A:} 06:00 AM -- 02:00 PM
    \item \textbf{Lab Shift B:} 02:00 PM -- 10:00 PM
    \item \textbf{Night Lab Shift:} 10:00 PM -- 06:00 AM
\end{itemize}

\section{Night-Shift and Women's Safety Rules}
\subsection{Mandatory Security Escort Window}
A verified security escort is mandatory for all women employees on drops departing after \textbf{9:00 PM} and on pickups scheduled before \textbf{6:00 AM}. Women finishing Lab Shift B (10:00 PM) are automatically assigned escorted cabs.

\subsection{Dual-Verification Check-In}
During the 9:00 PM -- 6:00 AM window, in addition to the physical security escort, every woman employee must complete a live \textbf{WhatsApp or SMS safe-arrival check-in within 10 minutes} of reaching home. If no confirmation is received within 10 minutes, the Transport Desk initiates a welfare call to the employee and their emergency contact.

\section{Roster Booking and Cancellation Cut-Off Timings}
\subsection{Booking Cut-Off}
Booking cut-off timings depend on the mode of transport:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Trunk Shuttles:} Must be booked at least \textbf{2 hours} before shift start.
    \item \textbf{Shared Home-Pickup Cabs:} Must be booked at least \textbf{6 hours} before shift start.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Cancellation Cut-Off}
Scheduled cab or shuttle seats may be cancelled without penalty up to \textbf{90 minutes} prior to the scheduled departure time. Late cancellations inside 90 minutes count as a No-Show (Section 7.1).

\section{Ad-Hoc Trips, Cost Caps, and Approvals}
\subsection{Ad-Hoc Cab Cost Caps}
Ad-hoc cab bookings for extended wet-lab experiments or urgent sample transfers are capped at \textbf{INR 1,500 per trip}, with a monthly reimbursement ceiling of \textbf{INR 7,500 per employee}.

\subsection{Ad-Hoc Trip Approval Authority}
Ad-hoc cabs require \textbf{dual approval}: first from the employee's \textbf{Lab Supervisor} and subsequently confirmation from the \textbf{Transport Desk} before dispatch.

\subsection{Airport Transfers}
Official airport transfers require approval from both the \textbf{Department Head} and the \textbf{Transport Desk}, must be booked at least \textbf{24 hours in advance}, and are capped at \textbf{INR 2,200 per trip}.

\section{Weekend and Holiday Pickups}
\subsection{Active Wet-Lab Shift Coverage}
Shared cab and shuttle services operate on \textbf{weekends (Saturday and Sunday) and public holidays} exclusively for personnel scheduled on active wet-lab or clinical-trial continuity shifts. Requests must be submitted at least \textbf{18 hours} prior to shift start. Administrative staff without lab duty are ineligible for weekend transport.

\section{Guest Travel Policy}
\subsection{Visiting Researchers and Auditors}
Transport for visiting researchers, regulatory auditors, and clinical partners is permitted in dedicated vendor vehicles only. The host must secure both a \textbf{Bio-Safety Visitor Pass} and \textbf{Transport Desk clearance at least 48 hours prior} to arrival.

\section{No-Show Penalties}
\subsection{No-Show Strike Policy}
Failure to board within 5 minutes of arrival or cancelling within 90 minutes of pickup constitutes a No-Show. Penalties accumulate over a rolling \textbf{90-day period}:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{1st No-Show:} Automated warning notification.
    \item \textbf{2nd No-Show:} Formal warning plus mandatory completion of a 15-minute Transport Etiquette e-module.
    \item \textbf{3rd No-Show:} Monetary fine of \textbf{INR 750} plus a \textbf{10-day suspension} of shared cab privileges.
\end{itemize}
