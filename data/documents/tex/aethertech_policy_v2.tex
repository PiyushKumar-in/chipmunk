\begin{abstract}
This revised document (\texttt{ATS-TP-2026-v2.0}) supersedes \texttt{ATS-TP-2026-v1.0} in its entirety effective July 1, 2026. It establishes updated rules for commute eligibility, night-shift women's safety, booking cut-offs, ad-hoc cost caps, airport approvals, weekend pickups, guest travel, and no-show penalties at AetherTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (Bengaluru Campus). For queries not covered herein, contact the AetherTech Transport Desk at \texttt{transport-desk@aethertech.example.com}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Eligibility Criteria}
\subsection{Band and Grade Eligibility}
Full-time employees in Bands \textbf{L1 through L5} are now eligible for company-provided shared roster cabs and shuttles. Employees in Band L6 and above qualify for a dedicated chauffeur-driven vehicle under the Executive Mobility Program. Interns and temporary contractors remain ineligible for daily roster transport unless explicitly sponsored by a Director (L7+).

\subsection{Distance and Geographic Radius}
Shared roster transport is available to eligible employees residing between \textbf{2 km and 40 km} (road distance) from the AetherTech Bengaluru Campus (Outer Ring Road). Employees residing within 2 km must commute independently; employees beyond 40 km may board from the nearest designated nodal pickup point within the 40 km boundary.

\subsection{Shift Eligibility}
Transport is provided across all three operational shifts:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{General Shift:} 09:00 AM -- 06:00 PM
    \item \textbf{Afternoon Shift:} 02:00 PM -- 11:00 PM
    \item \textbf{Night Shift:} 09:00 PM -- 06:00 AM
\end{itemize}

\section{Night-Shift and Women's Safety Rules}
\subsection{Mandatory Security Escort Window}
A trained, verified security guard (escort) is mandatory for all women employees on office-to-home drops departing after \textbf{7:30 PM} and on home-to-office pickups scheduled before \textbf{6:30 AM}. No shared cab carrying a woman employee during this expanded safety window may depart without a logged security escort.

\subsection{First-Pickup and Last-Drop Protocol}
During the 7:30 PM -- 6:30 AM safety window, a woman employee shall never be the first pickup or the last drop unless accompanied by a security escort. Drivers must complete a mandatory GPS panic-button functionality test prior to trip start and wait at the drop location until the employee confirms safe entry via the MoveInSync app.

\section{Roster Booking and Cancellation Cut-Off Timings}
\subsection{Booking Cut-Off}
Employees must submit or modify their daily roster cab request at least \textbf{6 hours} prior to the scheduled shift start time. Requests submitted inside the 6-hour window are treated as ad-hoc requests.

\subsection{Cancellation Cut-Off}
A scheduled pickup or drop may be cancelled without penalty up to \textbf{3 hours} prior to the scheduled pickup time. Cancellations made less than 3 hours before pickup are logged as a late cancellation and count toward the No-Show Strike Policy (Section 7.1).

\section{Ad-Hoc Trips, Cost Caps, and Approvals}
\subsection{Ad-Hoc Cab Cost Caps}
When roster cabs are unavailable due to unplanned overtime or urgent business needs, employees may book an ad-hoc cab or claim reimbursement via MoveInSync up to an updated cap of \textbf{INR 1,800 per trip}, subject to a monthly ceiling of \textbf{INR 9,000 per employee}. Expenses exceeding these caps require written pre-approval from the VP of Operations.

\subsection{Ad-Hoc Trip Approval Authority}
All ad-hoc cab bookings require digital approval from the employee's \textbf{Reporting Manager (Band L5 or above)} through the MoveInSync app prior to trip dispatch.

\subsection{Airport Transfers}
For official business travel airport transfers costing \textbf{INR 2,500 or less}, approval from the employee's \textbf{Reporting Manager} is sufficient. Airport transfers exceeding INR 2,500 require \textbf{Director (Band L7+)} approval, up to a hard cap of \textbf{INR 3,500 per trip}. Bookings must be raised at least 12 hours before travel.

\section{Weekend and Holiday Pickups}
\subsection{Eligibility and Advance Notice}
Weekend (Saturday and Sunday) and public holiday transport is available to employees in \textbf{Band L3 and above}, or employees formally assigned to an active P1/P2 on-call production support roster. Bookings must be submitted at least \textbf{12 hours} in advance and approved by the Reporting Manager.

\section{Guest Travel Policy}
\subsection{External Clients and Interview Candidates}
Company-arranged transport for external clients, vendors, and on-site interview candidates is permitted only when hosted by an employee in \textbf{Band L5 or above}. Guests are assigned dedicated vendor sedans and are never pooled into employee shared rosters.

\section{No-Show Penalties}
\subsection{No-Show Strike Policy}
Failure to board a scheduled cab within 5 minutes of arrival at the pickup point, or cancelling within 3 hours of pickup, constitutes a No-Show. Penalties within a rolling \textbf{30-day window} are enforced as follows:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{1st No-Show:} Automated warning email to the employee.
    \item \textbf{2nd No-Show:} Monetary penalty of \textbf{INR 500} deducted from monthly payroll and manager notification.
    \item \textbf{3rd No-Show:} Mandatory \textbf{14-day suspension} of roster transport privileges plus an additional \textbf{INR 500} administrative fine.
\end{itemize}
