\begin{abstract}
This document governs daily employee commute services, night-shift safety protocols, ad-hoc cab bookings, cost caps, weekend pickups, guest travel, and no-show penalties for AetherTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (Bengaluru Campus). All bookings are managed through the MoveInSync platform. For queries not covered in this policy, contact the AetherTech Transport Desk at \texttt{transport-desk@aethertech.example.com}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Eligibility Criteria}
\subsection{Band and Grade Eligibility}
Full-time employees in Bands L1 through L4 are eligible for company-provided shared roster cabs and shuttles. Employees in Band L5 and above are provided a dedicated chauffeur-driven vehicle under the Executive Mobility Program and are excluded from shared roster bookings. Interns and temporary contractors are ineligible for daily roster transport unless explicitly sponsored by a Director (L7+).

\subsection{Distance and Geographic Radius}
Shared roster transport is available to eligible employees residing between \textbf{3 km and 35 km} (road distance) from the AetherTech Bengaluru Campus (Outer Ring Road). Employees residing within 3 km are expected to commute independently; employees beyond 35 km may board from the nearest designated nodal pickup point within the 35 km boundary.

\subsection{Shift Eligibility}
Transport is provided across all three operational shifts:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{General Shift:} 09:00 AM -- 06:00 PM
    \item \textbf{Afternoon Shift:} 02:00 PM -- 11:00 PM
    \item \textbf{Night Shift:} 09:00 PM -- 06:00 AM
\end{itemize}

\section{Night-Shift and Women's Safety Rules}
\subsection{Mandatory Security Escort Window}
A trained, verified security guard (escort) is mandatory for all women employees on office-to-home drops departing after \textbf{8:00 PM} and on home-to-office pickups scheduled before \textbf{6:00 AM}. No shared cab carrying a woman employee during this window may depart the campus without a logged security escort.

\subsection{First-Pickup and Last-Drop Protocol}
During the 8:00 PM -- 6:00 AM safety window, a woman employee shall never be the first pickup or the last drop unless accompanied by a security escort. Upon reaching home, the driver must wait until the employee safely enters the residential premises and confirms arrival via the MoveInSync mobile application.

\section{Roster Booking and Cancellation Cut-Off Timings}
\subsection{Booking Cut-Off}
Employees must submit or modify their daily roster cab request at least \textbf{4 hours} prior to the scheduled shift start time. Requests submitted inside the 4-hour window are treated as ad-hoc requests and are not guaranteed shared cab allocation.

\subsection{Cancellation Cut-Off}
A scheduled pickup or drop may be cancelled without penalty up to \textbf{2 hours} prior to the scheduled pickup time. Cancellations made less than 2 hours before pickup are logged as a late cancellation and count toward the No-Show Strike Policy (Section 7.1).

\section{Ad-Hoc Trips, Cost Caps, and Approvals}
\subsection{Ad-Hoc Cab Cost Caps}
When roster cabs are unavailable due to unplanned overtime or urgent business needs, employees may book an ad-hoc cab or claim reimbursement via MoveInSync up to a cap of \textbf{INR 1,200 per trip}, subject to a monthly ceiling of \textbf{INR 6,000 per employee}. Expenses exceeding these caps are borne by the employee unless pre-approved in writing by the VP of Operations.

\subsection{Ad-Hoc Trip Approval Authority}
All ad-hoc cab bookings require digital approval from the employee's \textbf{Reporting Manager (Band L5 or above)} through the MoveInSync app prior to trip dispatch.

\subsection{Airport Transfers}
Airport pickup and drop cabs for official business travel require pre-approval from a \textbf{Director (Band L7+)} at least 12 hours before flight departure or arrival. Airport transfer reimbursements are capped at \textbf{INR 2,500 per trip}.

\section{Weekend and Holiday Pickups}
\subsection{Eligibility and Advance Notice}
Weekend (Saturday and Sunday) and public holiday transport is restricted to employees in \textbf{Band L4 and above}, or employees formally assigned to an active P1/P2 on-call production support roster. Bookings must be submitted at least \textbf{24 hours} in advance and approved by the Reporting Manager.

\section{Guest Travel Policy}
\subsection{External Clients and Interview Candidates}
Company-arranged transport for external clients, vendors, and on-site interview candidates is permitted only when hosted by an employee in \textbf{Band L5 or above}. Guests are assigned dedicated vendor sedans and are never pooled into employee shared rosters.

\section{No-Show Penalties}
\subsection{No-Show Strike Policy}
Failure to board a scheduled cab within 5 minutes of arrival at the pickup point, or cancelling within 2 hours of pickup, constitutes a No-Show. Penalties within a rolling \textbf{30-day window} are enforced as follows:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{1st No-Show:} Automated warning email to the employee.
    \item \textbf{2nd No-Show:} Monetary penalty of \textbf{INR 300} deducted from monthly payroll and manager notification.
    \item \textbf{3rd No-Show:} Mandatory \textbf{7-day suspension} of roster transport privileges.
\end{itemize}
